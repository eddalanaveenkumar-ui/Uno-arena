Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information UNO Arena collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using UNO Arena you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account data: a guest account is created automatically when you start playing. You can add an email address and password to secure it. We store your display name, avatar, and game statistics (matches, wins, losses, streaks).
  • Game data: your virtual coin balance, coin history, and match history.
  • Technical data: your session token, stored in your browser so you stay signed in, and basic server logs such as IP address and time of access.
  • Advertising data: cookies and similar technologies used by advertising partners (see below).

How we use information

We use this information to run the game, keep your account and progress, prevent abuse, show the leaderboard, answer support requests, and show advertising.

Advertising

UNO Arena shows advertisements from third parties, including Google AdSense and Adsterra. These partners may use cookies and device identifiers to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn about Google’s use of data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and manage personalised ads in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of certain third-party cookies at aboutads.info.

Service providers

We use Supabase to store accounts and game data and to authenticate users, and Render to host the game server. These providers process data on our behalf.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in. Advertising partners may set cookies as described above. You can clear cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings.

Children

UNO Arena is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and your rights

We keep account data while your account exists. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by contacting us at eddalanaveenkumar@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
