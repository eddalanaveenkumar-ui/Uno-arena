About UNO Arena

UNO Arena is a free online version of the classic UNO card game. Play against computer bots at any time, or create a room and play in real time with friends on your phone, tablet or computer.

What you can do

  • Play UNO against bots, with the official card rules including Skip, Reverse, Draw Two, Wild and Wild Draw Four.
  • Create or join a room with a code and play with friends.
  • Track your matches, wins, streaks and rank on your profile, and compare yourself on the leaderboard.
  • Earn virtual coins for playing and by watching short ads. Coins have no cash value.

Who runs UNO Arena

UNO Arena is an independent project. For questions, see our contact page.
